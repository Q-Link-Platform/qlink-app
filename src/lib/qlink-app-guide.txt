[SECTION: ENCRYPTED_CHAT_AND_E2EE]
End-to-End Cryptographic Architecture:
• True Zero-Knowledge E2EE: All private messages and attachments are encrypted directly inside the sender's browser using ephemeral Curve25519 key negotiation and AES-GCM-256 ciphers before transmission.
• Zero Server Interception: The backend server only routes encrypted binary cipher blobs. No plaintext messages or secret private keys are ever stored or accessible on the server.
• Status Receipts:
  - 🕒 Sending (Local queue)
  - ✓ Single Grey Tick (Sent & Delivered to Relay)
  - ✓✓ Double Cyan Ticks (Delivered to Peer's Device)
  - 🔵 Blue Highlighted Ticks (Read by Recipient)

[SECTION: LIVE_SYNC_AND_EDITS]
Live Message Editing & Peer Synchronization:
• How to Edit: Hover over any of your sent messages in chat and click the "✏️ Edit" pencil icon (or double tap on mobile). Modify the text and hit Enter or click "✓ Save".
• Instant Peer Synchronization: The edit is encrypted with a new ephemeral tag and broadcasts across the WebSocket/SSE channel in <50ms. Both screens update live with an "(edited)" indicator.
• Cancel Edit: Press Escape or click the "✕ Cancel" pill above the input console.

[SECTION: MEDIA_VOICE_ATTACHMENTS]
Voice Audio Notes & Media Sharing:
• Voice Messaging: Tap the 🎙️ Microphone button in the chat console to start voice recording. An active animated waveform displays recording duration. Tap the ✓ Send button to send the encrypted audio clip, or ✕ to cancel.
• File Attachments: Click the 📎 Paperclip button (or open the 3-dots more menu `...` on compact screens) to attach documents, PDFs, images, or code files up to 50MB with instant E2EE client encryption.
• Image & Video Media: Drag and drop media directly onto the chat window or select via the Media picker.

[SECTION: Q_BEACON_EMERGENCY]

[SECTION: UI_NAVIGATION_AND_TROUBLESHOOTING]
UI Navigation & Common Problem Resolution:
• Opening Q-AI Copilot: Tap the glowing cyan AI pill or floating orb on the right side of the chat panel. It opens a docked 360px sidecar without disrupting your active chat.
• Switching Chats: Click on any friend in the left sidebar to switch conversations. Unread badges indicate pending messages.
• Mobile View Navigation: On small screens, use the top chevron/back button `‹` to return from the chat view to the friend directory.
• Reconnecting on Network Drop: If your connection indicator shows red ("Disconnected"), Q-Link's auto-reconnect engine will automatically poll and re-establish the encrypted session within 3 seconds. You can also click the status dot to force a manual reconnect.
• Audio / Mic Permissions: If voice recording fails, verify that microphone access is allowed in your browser address bar permissions (lock icon).
